## Wammies With Friends 2.12.1

### Play: the board plays like a game

- **The page is the game.** With a game open, the board fills the window between the two armies. The selected unit's actions sit in a bar under the board, and the battle log is a drawer (the log button, or L). Tools you use less are under More.
- **See what you can do before you do it.**
  - **Shooting:** units you can shoot are outlined and the rest are dimmed. Clicking one you can't shoot says why, and hovering a weapon shows its range.
  - **Attack preview:** shown in the action bar, and when you hover an enemy: about how many models would die, the chance to kill one, to wipe the unit out, and to kill its leader.
  - **Charging:** before the roll, each enemy within 12" shows its distance and your chance of making it.
  - **Moving:** while you drag, the ground too close to the enemy is shaded, and the unit turns red with the reason before you drop it.
- **Results on the board.**
  - A banner announces each phase.
  - Attacks show their dice and attacks › hits › wounds › killed over the board, with numbers rising from the target.
  - Models killed this phase stay marked where they fell.
  - Units have nameplates with health bars, and objectives show who holds them right now.
- **Smoother turns.**
  - Next phase warns you when units can still act.
  - Tab picks the next unit that can act.
  - Undo says what it will undo.
  - Keyboard shortcuts (? lists them).
  - A tactical view without the battle mat, and a button to turn the board.
  - Optional sounds.
- **Between turns.**
  - Hot-seat games show a "pass to…" screen when the other player is to act.
  - Coming back to an online game shows what the other player did, step by step on the board.
  - "Notify me" gives a notice when it's your move.
  - The games list says whose move it is, the round and the score.
  - The new-game form previews the battlefield.
- **The main game buttons are cream, not orange,** so orange always means Player 1.

### Phones

- **The right layout for the device.** The app works out whether it's on a phone, a tablet or a desktop from what the device can do, so a phone held sideways is still a phone. You can choose a layout yourself on the Setup, Account or Play page.
- **Play has its own phone game screen.**
  - The board fills the screen, turned upright when you hold the phone upright.
  - The unit's actions sit under the board, or beside it when you hold the phone sideways.
  - The armies and the log open as sheets.
  - A dragged model sits above your finger, and a move waits for Confirm.
  - Fire and Next phase need a short hold, so a slip doesn't end your phase.
  - Double-tap zooms in on a unit.
- **Every page on a phone:**
  - A tab bar at the bottom, with tips on a tap or long-press.
  - Sheets you drag down to close.
  - A Ctrl+K command palette on desktop.
  - A tidier, more consistent look throughout.
