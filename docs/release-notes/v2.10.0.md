## Wammies With Friends 2.10.0: phones

Every page now works on a phone, from 360px wide, and by touch: open a self-hosted copy in your phone's browser.

### New

- **A menu button** holds the page links on narrow screens.
- **Finger-sized controls on touch screens:** buttons, dropdowns, fields and tick boxes. Pop-ups fill the screen, with their buttons at the bottom.
- **Tables that fit:** lists (army units, factions, rules, stratagems, players) become cards on a phone; stat tables scroll sideways with the name kept in view.
- **Tap ⓘ for explanations** that only showed on mouse hover before, such as when to tick a rule and what an army's unit still has to fix.
- **Analytics on a phone:** the matrix shows one attacker at a time against every defender, with previous and next buttons and the army's total; a switch brings back the full grid. A cell's detail fills the screen.
- **Play on a phone:**
  - The board is on top, with tabs below for each army and the battle log.
  - A unit you select opens in a panel at the bottom of the screen, and the board scrolls into view above it.
  - Pinch to zoom and two fingers to pan; zoom buttons too.
  - Press and hold a model for its datasheet card.
  - A tap won't nudge a unit, and a tap just off a small base still picks the model.
  - **One model** (the person button above the board) moves a single model, and Spin model buttons turn it on the spot, as Shift-drag and Shift + mouse wheel do with a mouse.
- **Add it to your home screen:** the app has an icon and opens without the browser's bars.

Nothing changes on a desktop, except that the header's links wrap onto a second line when the window is too narrow for one.

Download the zip for your system below, unzip it and run `WammiesWithFriends` (`.exe` on Windows). Your data carries over.

**If you self-host with Docker:** `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`.
