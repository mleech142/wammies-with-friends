## Wammies With Friends 2.11.0: fixes from testing

### Fixed

- **Force disposition kept** (#28): opening a saved army showed its first force disposition instead of the one it was saved with, and saving it again lost the right one. Share codes now carry it too.
- **Adding units on a phone** (#26): the Armies page no longer jumps when you add a unit from the list; the list stays where it was (iPhones moved it before).
- **Abilities for another detachment** (#27): Chaos Daemons units showed their Shadow Legion ability whichever detachment was chosen. An ability BSData gives only with some detachments now shows only with them, in the Match view and on Play's unit cards. This fixes about 20 abilities in other factions too, such as Thousand Sons' Mortal Sorcery and Votann's Guerrilla Adepts.
- **Daemon Prince allegiances** (#29): choosing Khorne, Nurgle, Slaanesh or Tzeentch now gives its bonus as well as its keyword: +2 S on the Hellforged weapons, +1 T, 10" Move or 6 attacks on the Infernal cannon. This applies to the Chaos Space Marines Daemon Princes too, and wherever the unit is used: the Armies page, the Match view, Analytics and Play.

### Removed

- **Saved scenarios** (#25): the Scenarios page and the Simulator's Save Scenario button are gone. Analytics' saved comparisons stay.
- **Afflicted on Analytics** (#25): Analytics no longer offers Death Guard's Afflicted choices. The Simulator and Play still use them.

The app reads your BSData files again on its first start, which takes a little longer once.

Download the zip for your system below, unzip it and run `WammiesWithFriends` (`.exe` on Windows). Your data carries over.

**If you self-host with Docker:** `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`.
