## Wammies With Friends 3.1.0: wargear points

- **Wargear adds its points.** Each weapon or item a datasheet prices ("per Multi-melta: 5 pts") is added to the unit's points for every one beyond the datasheet's own loadout. For example, a Leman Russ's hull lascannon is free and each lascannon sponson costs 5. The Wargear popup shows "+5 pts each" beside each priced row and the total the wargear adds. Army totals and the unit's points in Analytics include it.
- **Wargear that isn't a weapon** (storm shields, banners, the Orbital Comms Array) has its own row in the Wargear popup, with a count. Share codes carry it.

Download the zip for your system below, unzip it and run `WammiesWithFriends` (`.exe` on Windows). Your data carries over.

**If you self-host with Docker:** `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`.
